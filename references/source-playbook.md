# Source Playbook

## Priority

1. Official exchange, regulator, or company IR source
2. Official PDF hosted on exchange or disclosure platform
3. Official notice mirrored by recognized disclosure media
4. Media summary only as locator or fallback

## A-share issuer sources

- `cninfo.com.cn`: full prospectus PDFs, quarterly reports, annual reports, shareholder and related-party disclosures
- `sse.com.cn` / `szse.cn`: exchange notices, inquiry, pricing, issuance, and risk announcements
- Company IR pages when easier to navigate than exchange mirrors

Key A-share IPO documents:

- 招股说明书 / 招股意向书
- 发行安排及初步询价公告
- 投资风险特别公告
- 战略配售相关公告
- 上市公告书 if already listed

Do not ask about cornerstone investors for A-share unless the specific structure explicitly exists. Usually the correct object is strategic placement and lockup.

## Hong Kong issuer sources

- `hkexnews.hk` / HKEX disclosure pages
- Hearing-posted documents
- Prospectus
- Allotment results
- Cornerstone investor section

Key Hong Kong IPO documents:

- 聆讯后资料集 / PHIP
- Prospectus
- Allotment results
- Cornerstone investor disclosure
- Lockup and existing shareholder sale restrictions

## Peer sources

Use the freshest disclosed period:

- Latest quarter first
- Then latest annual or interim

For each peer, prefer:

- Exchange filing or company PDF
- IR presentation or investor relations record
- Only then media summaries

## Policy sources

Use only official policy sources where possible:

- `nmpa.gov.cn`
- `miit.gov.cn`
- `cac.gov.cn`
- `ndrc.gov.cn`
- sector regulator and ministry pages

For environmental or capacity-heavy issuers, look for:

- Energy consumption policy
- Environmental approval burdens
- Import dependency constraints
- Export control or tariff pressure
